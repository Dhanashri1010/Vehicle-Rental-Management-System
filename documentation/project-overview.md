# PedalPal - Project Overview

## 1. Project Title & Tagline
- **Project Name:** PedalPal – Bike & Scooter Rental Platform
- **Tagline:** "Your Ride. Your Way."
- **Academic Context:** College Major Project - First Project Review Preparation
- **Target Audience:** College students, IT professionals, daily commuters, tourists, and vehicle owners across Pune, Maharashtra.

## 2. Problem Statement
In tier-1 and educational hubs like Pune, students and young professionals face severe personal mobility hurdles:
1. **Public Transit Constraints:** Uneven coverage and crowded feeder buses in rapidly developing tech corridors (Hinjewadi, Baner, Kharadi, Wakad).
2. **High Auto/Cab Fares:** Daily surge pricing on app cabs adds financial stress.
3. **Vehicle Ownership Overhead:** Purchasing a new two-wheeler incurs high capital expense, depreciation, annual insurance, and maintenance.
4. **Existing Rental Drawbacks:** Fragmented offline rental shops often demand cash deposits, withhold deposits arbitrarily, enforce obscure penalty terms, and lack transparent digital records.

## 3. Project Objectives
PedalPal bridges this gap with a modern, transparent two-wheeler rental marketplace:
1. **Convenient Discovery:** Search and filter clean, serviced bikes and scooters by Pune locality, category, and fuel type.
2. **Predictable & Honest Pricing:** Transparent hourly, daily, weekly, and monthly rates with a clearly stated 100% refundable safety deposit.
3. **Safety & Assurance:** Every listed vehicle includes a clean, sanitized helmet and 24/7 Pune roadside breakdown support.
4. **Peer-to-Peer Owner Hub:** Local bike owners can list idle two-wheelers, earn passive income, review incoming requests, and monitor monthly payouts.
5. **Centralized Administration:** Comprehensive admin control over customer and owner verification, vehicle listing moderation, booking lifecycles, and operational reports.

## 4. Vehicle Scope
The platform specializes strictly in two-wheelers:
- **Petrol Scooters:** Honda Activa 6G, TVS Jupiter 125, Suzuki Access 125, Yamaha RayZR 125, Hero Pleasure Plus.
- **Petrol Motorcycles:** Royal Enfield Classic 350, Royal Enfield Hunter 350, Bajaj Pulsar 150, Hero Splendor Plus, Yamaha FZ-S V3, KTM Duke 200, Honda CB Shine 125, TVS Apache RTR 160 4V, Bajaj Avenger Cruise 220.
- **Electric Scooters:** Ather 450X Gen 3, Ola S1 Pro Gen 2, TVS iQube Electric S, Bajaj Chetak Electric, Hero Vida V1 Pro.
- **Electric Motorcycles:** Revolt RV400, Tork Kratos R, Ultraviolette F77 Mach 2.
*(All car rentals have been eliminated in accordance with project specialization).*

## 5. Three Distinct User Portals
1. **Customer Portal:** Explore bikes, compare up to 3 vehicles, calculate tariffs, reserve with doorstep delivery or self-pickup, manage trips, and track safety refunds.
2. **Bike Owner Portal:** Dedicated owner dashboard, listing management, adding new bikes with image previews, accepting/rejecting booking requests, and viewing payout statistics.
3. **Admin Portal:** System overview, customer and owner moderation, vehicle approval/rejection, booking audit logs, and performance metrics.
